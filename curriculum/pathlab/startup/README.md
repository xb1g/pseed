# Startup Path Curriculum

**Format**: Solo individual learning · 6 days · ~30–45 min/day
**Audience**: Grade 9–11 (14–17 years old)
**Goal**: Explore what it's like to build a startup from scratch — from spotting a problem to pitching your idea

---

## Narrative Frame

You've been accepted into **LaunchPad**, a 6-day virtual startup accelerator. Each day you'll tackle one phase of building a real startup. By Day 6, you'll have a complete pitch deck and a simulated investor score.

Your progress is tracked through **Founder XP** and a **Startup Health Score** — make smart moves and your startup thrives; make bad calls and you'll need to pivot.

---

## Day 1 — 💡 Spot the Problem

**Theme**: Every great startup starts with a real problem

**The Hook**
> "The best businesses don't sell products — they kill pain points. Today, you become a Problem Hunter."

### Learn (5 min)
- Short content: What makes a problem worth solving? (frequency × intensity × market size)
- Examples: Airbnb (can't afford hotels), Grab (can't find a safe ride), Canva (design tools are too hard)
- Key concept: **Problem-Solution Fit** — don't start with an idea, start with a pain

### Do (20 min)

**Activity: Problem Safari** 🔍
1. List 5 problems you personally experienced this week
   - Examples to spark thinking: school, commuting, studying, shopping, social media, health
2. For each problem, rate:
   - **Pain level** (1–5): How annoying is this?
   - **Frequency** (1–5): How often does it happen?
   - **Who else has it?** (just me / my friends / everyone)
3. Pick your #1 problem — the one with highest pain × frequency × reach

**Activity: Problem Statement** ✍️
Write your problem in this format:
> **[Who]** struggles with **[problem]** because **[reason]**, which causes **[consequence]**.

Example:
> **High school students** struggle with **finding reliable study groups** because **they don't know who's serious**, which causes **wasted time and bad grades**.

### Deliver
- [ ] Problem Safari worksheet (5 problems rated)
- [ ] 1 chosen problem with a clear problem statement

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| List 5 problems | +50 |
| Rate all problems | +30 |
| Write problem statement | +50 |
| Problem scores ≥4 on both pain & frequency | +20 bonus |

**Startup Health Score**: Starts at 50/100

## Day 2 — 🕵️ Who's Your Customer?

**Theme**: You're not building for "everyone" — find your exact person

**The Hook**
> "If your answer to 'who's your customer?' is 'everyone' — you have no customer. Today you'll find your ONE person."

### Learn (5 min)
- What is a **Customer Persona**? (a fictional character that represents your ideal user)
- Why niching down matters — Instagram started with photographers, Facebook started with Harvard
- Key concept: **Early Adopters** — the people who are SO frustrated they'll try anything

### Do (20 min)

**Activity: Build Your Persona** 🧑‍🎤
Create a detailed profile of your ideal first customer:
- Name (make one up)
- Age, grade, school type
- Daily routine (morning → night)
- What apps do they use?
- What frustrates them most about your problem?
- What do they currently do to solve it? (the "hack" or workaround)
- How much time/money do they waste on this problem?

**Activity: Assumption Check** ⚡
List 3 assumptions you're making about your customer:
1. I assume they care about [___] because [___]
2. I assume they currently do [___] to solve this
3. I assume they'd pay/use [___] if it existed

For each assumption, rate your confidence (guess / somewhat sure / very sure)

**Activity: Quick Validation** 📱
- Write 3 questions you'd ask a real person to test your assumptions
- Think: open-ended questions, not yes/no
- Good: "Tell me about the last time you had this problem"
- Bad: "Would you use my app?" (everyone says yes, nobody means it)

### Deliver
- [ ] Customer persona card (complete profile)
- [ ] 3 assumptions with confidence ratings
- [ ] 3 validation questions

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Complete persona card | +50 |
| List 3 assumptions | +30 |
| Write 3 validation questions | +40 |
| All questions are open-ended (no yes/no) | +20 bonus |

**Startup Health**: +5 if persona is specific (not "everyone age 15–18")

## Day 3 — 🧩 The Business Model

**Theme**: A cool idea without a business model is just a hobby

**The Hook**
> "You've found a real problem and a real customer. But how does this become a business? Today you figure out how your startup actually works — and makes money."

### Learn (5 min)
- Introduction to the **Lean Canvas** (simplified, 1-page business model)
- Revenue models teens can understand:
  - **Freemium**: Free basic, pay for premium (Spotify, games)
  - **Subscription**: Monthly fee (Netflix, gym)
  - **Marketplace**: Take a cut of transactions (Grab, Shopee)
  - **Advertising**: Free for users, sell attention (Instagram, YouTube)
  - **One-time purchase**: Pay once (apps, courses)
- Key concept: **Unit Economics** — does selling 1 unit make you money or lose money?

### Do (25 min)

**Activity: Lean Canvas** 📋
Fill out a simplified Lean Canvas for your startup:

| Box | Question | Your Answer |
|-----|----------|-------------|
| **Problem** | Top 3 pain points (from Day 1) | |
| **Customer** | Who exactly (from Day 2) | |
| **Solution** | What do you build to fix it? | |
| **Value Prop** | Why would someone choose YOU? (1 sentence) | |
| **Revenue** | How do you make money? Pick a model | |
| **Pricing** | How much? Why? | |
| **Channels** | How do customers find you? | |
| **Cost** | What do you need to spend money on? | |

**Activity: Quick Math 💰**
Do a napkin calculation:
- If you charge **฿[X]** per month
- And you get **[Y]** customers in month 1
- Your revenue = ฿[X] × [Y] = ฿___
- Your main costs = ฿___
- Profit/Loss = ฿___

Are you making money or burning cash? If burning, what needs to change?

**Decision Point: Choose Your Path** 🔀
Pick one (this affects your Day 4):
- **A) Digital Product** — app, website, or online tool
- **B) Physical Product/Service** — something in the real world
- **C) Content/Community** — media, education, or community-based

### Deliver
- [ ] Completed Lean Canvas
- [ ] Napkin math calculation
- [ ] Path choice (A, B, or C)

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Complete Lean Canvas | +60 |
| Napkin math (revenue > 0) | +30 |
| Napkin math (actually realistic) | +20 bonus |
| Choose path | +10 |

**Startup Health**: +10 if value prop is clear and unique, -5 if pricing makes no sense

## Day 4 — 🎨 Build Something Real

**Theme**: Stop planning, start making — even if it's ugly

**The Hook**
> "Founders don't wait until it's perfect. They ship ugly things fast and learn. Today you build your MVP — Minimum Viable Product. Emphasis on MINIMUM."

### Learn (5 min)
- What is an **MVP**? The smallest thing you can build to test if your idea works
- Famous ugly MVPs:
  - Dropbox: Just a video showing the concept
  - Zappos: Bought shoes from stores and shipped them manually
  - Buffer: A single landing page with a pricing button
- Key concept: **Build → Measure → Learn** loop
- You're not building a product. You're building a **learning machine**.

### Do (25 min)

**Activity: Choose Your MVP Type** 🛠️
Based on your Day 3 path choice, pick ONE:

| MVP Type | What you make | Best for |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Landing Page** | A 1-page website describing your product with a "sign up" button | Digital products |
| **Storyboard** | A 6-panel comic/sketch showing a customer using your product | Any product |
| **Fake Ad** | Design a social media ad for your product (image + caption) | Marketing-heavy ideas |
| **Prototype Walkthrough** | Draw 4–6 screens of your app/website on paper | App/website ideas |
| **Explainer Script** | Write a 60-second pitch video script | Content/service ideas |

**Activity: Build It** 🔨
Create your chosen MVP. Guidelines:
- **Landing Page**: Use any free tool (Google Slides, Canva, or just write the text)
  - Must include: headline, problem statement, how it works (3 steps), call-to-action
- **Storyboard**: 6 panels, stick figures are fine
  - Panel 1: Character has the problem → Panel 6: Problem solved with your product
- **Fake Ad**: Design 1 Instagram/TikTok ad
  - Image/visual + caption + hashtags + call-to-action
- **Prototype**: Draw 4–6 screens on paper or digitally
  - Show: landing screen → main feature → key interaction → success state
- **Explainer Script**: 60-second script
  - Structure: Hook (5s) → Problem (10s) → Solution (20s) → How it works (15s) → CTA (10s)

**Activity: The Mom Test** 🤔
Write down: If you showed this MVP to a stranger...
1. What's the FIRST question they'd ask?
2. What would confuse them?
3. What would make them say "oh that's cool"?

### Deliver
- [ ] Chosen MVP type
- [ ] The actual MVP (photo, file, or written description)
- [ ] Mom Test — 3 answers

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Choose MVP type | +10 |
| Complete the MVP | +80 |
| Answer Mom Test questions | +30 |
| MVP is creative / high effort | +30 bonus |

**Startup Health**: +15 if MVP clearly connects to the problem from Day 1, -10 if it looks like a completely different idea (scope creep!)

## Day 5 — 📣 Get Your First Users

**Theme**: Building it is 20% of the work. Getting people to care is 80%.

**The Hook**
> "You've built something. Cool. Nobody cares yet. Today you learn the hardest lesson in business: attention is the real currency."

### Learn (5 min)
- **Growth channels** that actually work for startups (not TV ads):
  - Social media (organic content, reels, stories)
  - Word of mouth / referrals
  - Communities (Discord, LINE groups, Reddit)
  - Partnerships (collaborate with someone who has your audience)
  - Content marketing (teach something valuable for free)
- Key concept: **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) — how much does it cost to get 1 customer?
- The **AARRR Funnel** (Pirate Metrics):
  - **A**cquisition: How do they find you?
  - **A**ctivation: Do they have a good first experience?
  - **R**etention: Do they come back?
  - **R**evenue: Do they pay?
  - **R**eferral: Do they tell friends?

### Do (25 min)

**Activity: Growth Plan** 📈
Plan how you'd get your first 100 users:

| Phase | Users | Channel | What you do |
|-------|-------|---------|------------|
| First 10 | 0→10 | ??? | ??? |
| Next 40 | 10→50 | ??? | ??? |
| Next 50 | 50→100 | ??? | ??? |

Rules:
- First 10 must be from **direct outreach** (no ads)
- You have a budget of ฿0 for the first 50 users
- After 50 users, you can spend ฿500 max

**Activity: Create 1 Piece of Content** 📱
Make ONE social media post for your startup:
- Pick a platform (Instagram, TikTok, X, LINE)
- Write the caption/script
- Describe the visual (or sketch it)
- Include a hook in the first line
- Must feel authentic, not salesy (teens can smell ads from a mile away)

**Activity: Decision Scenario** 🎮
Choose your response to this situation:

> It's Week 2. You have 47 users. Growth is slowing down. Your friend says "just pay for Instagram ads." You have ฿2,000 in your startup budget. What do you do?

Options:
- **A)** Spend ฿1,500 on Instagram ads targeting your audience
- **B)** Spend ฿0 — instead, DM 20 of your current users and ask why they signed up, then make your product better
- **C)** Spend ฿500 on a small collab with a micro-influencer who matches your audience
- **D)** Your own strategy: [write it out]

Explain your reasoning in 3–4 sentences.

### Deliver
- [ ] Growth plan (first 100 users)
- [ ] 1 social media post (caption + visual description)
- [ ] Decision scenario — choice + reasoning

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Growth plan for 100 users | +50 |
| Create social content | +40 |
| Decision scenario answered | +30 |
| Growth plan doesn't rely on ads for first 50 | +20 bonus |
| Reasoning shows understanding of CAC | +20 bonus |

**Startup Health**: +10 if growth plan is specific (names real platforms, real communities), -5 if plan is just "post on social media"

## Day 6 — 🎤 Pitch Day

**Theme**: The grand finale — sell your vision

**The Hook**
> "Every startup lives or dies by its pitch. Investors, customers, partners — everyone needs to believe in YOUR story. Today, you make them believe."

### Learn (5 min)
- Anatomy of a great pitch:
  1. **Hook** — a surprising fact, bold statement, or relatable story
  2. **Problem** — make them FEEL the pain
  3. **Solution** — your startup (keep it simple!)
  4. **How it works** — 3 steps max
  5. **Traction** — what you've done so far (MVP, users, learnings)
  6. **Business model** — how you make money
  7. **The Ask** — what do you need? (funding, users, feedback)
- Watch: 1-minute examples of great teen/student pitches (curated links)
- Key concept: **Storytelling > Statistics** — investors invest in founders, not spreadsheets

### Do (30 min)

**Activity: Build Your Pitch Deck** 🎯
Create a 7-slide pitch deck (use Google Slides, Canva, or just write it out):

| Slide | Content | Tips |
|-------|---------|------|
| 1. **Title** | Startup name, tagline, your name | Make the tagline memorable |
| 2. **Problem** | The pain point (use your Day 1 work) | Use a real story or stat |
| 3. **Solution** | What you're building | Show, don't tell — use your MVP |
| 4. **How It Works** | 3-step walkthrough | Keep it dead simple |
| 5. **Market** | Who needs this & how big is the opportunity | Use your persona + napkin math |
| 6. **Business Model** | How you make money + growth plan | Show you've thought about this |
| 7. **The Ask** | What you need to make this real | Be specific |

**Activity: Write Your 60-Second Pitch Script** 🎙️
Write exactly what you'd SAY if you had 60 seconds in front of an investor:
- Hook (10 sec)
- Problem (10 sec)
- Solution (15 sec)
- How it works (10 sec)
- Why now / why you (10 sec)
- The ask (5 sec)

**Activity: Self-Grade Your Journey** 📊
Reflect on your 6-day startup journey:

| Question | Your Answer |
|----------|-------------|
| What's the most surprising thing you learned? | |
| What was the hardest part? | |
| Would you actually want to build this startup for real? Why/why not? | |
| Which business role excited you most? (product, marketing, finance, sales) | |
| On a scale of 1–10, how interested are you in entrepreneurship now? | |

### Deliver
- [ ] 7-slide pitch deck
- [ ] 60-second pitch script
- [ ] Self-reflection answers

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Complete pitch deck (all 7 slides) | +80 |
| Write pitch script | +50 |
| Self-reflection complete | +30 |
| Pitch has a strong hook (not "Hi, my name is...") | +20 bonus |
| Journey is coherent Day 1→6 (same problem throughout) | +30 bonus |

**Final Startup Health Score**: Calculated from all 6 days

## 📐 Design Notes

### Content Principles
1. **Show, don't lecture** — examples > theory, always
2. **Real companies, not textbook cases** — use brands teens know (Grab, Shopee, TikTok, Canva)
3. **Imperfect is fine** — emphasize that ugly MVPs and wrong assumptions are part of the process
4. **No right answers** — every decision scenario has trade-offs, not correct answers
5. **Career breadcrumbs** — each day subtly exposes a different business career (product manager, marketer, financial analyst, designer, salesperson)

### Career Exposure by Day

| Day | Primary career exposed | Secondary |
|-----|----------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Product Manager | UX Researcher |
| 2 | UX Researcher | Data Analyst |
| 3 | Business Analyst / CFO | Strategy Consultant |
| 4 | Product Designer | Software Developer |
| 5 | Growth Marketer | Community Manager |
| 6 | CEO / Founder | Sales / BD |

### Tone Guide
- Talk TO them, not AT them
- Use "you" not "students" or "learners"
- Casual but not cringe — no "bussin" or "no cap" (it'll be outdated by launch)
- Okay to use light humor and memes as references
- Challenge them — don't dumb it down, teens rise to the level of expectation
