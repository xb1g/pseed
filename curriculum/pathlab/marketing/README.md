# Marketing Path Curriculum

**Format**: Solo individual learning · 6 days · ~30–45 min/day
**Audience**: Grade 9–11 (14–17 years old)
**Goal**: Learn how marketing actually works — from understanding why people buy to running your own mini-campaign
**Prerequisite**: None (but connects naturally if you've done the Startup path)

---

## Narrative Frame

Welcome to **Signal Studio**, a 6-day marketing bootcamp where you learn to make people care about something. You'll pick a product or brand to market — it can be your startup from the Startup path, a real brand you love, or a completely made-up one.

By Day 6, you'll have a complete marketing strategy, a portfolio of content you created, and real data from a mini-campaign.

Your progress is tracked through **Marketer XP** and a **Brand Power Score** — smart moves build your brand; bad calls tank your reach.

## Day 1 — 🧠 Why People Buy

**Theme**: Marketing isn't about shouting louder. It's about understanding brains.

**The Hook**
> "You've been manipulated today. Probably 50+ times. Every ad, every notification, every 'limited time offer' — someone designed that to hack your brain. Today, you learn how."

### Learn (5 min)
- **Consumer psychology basics** — people buy with emotions, then justify with logic
- 5 psychological triggers that drive purchases:
  - **Social proof**: "Everyone's using it" (reviews, follower counts)
  - **Scarcity**: "Only 3 left!" (limited drops, countdown timers)
  - **Reciprocity**: "Here's something free" → you feel you owe them
  - **Authority**: "Recommended by experts" (certifications, endorsements)
  - **FOMO**: "You'll miss out" (stories that disappear, flash sales)
- Examples teens know:
  - Nike: Sells identity, not shoes
  - Apple: Sells status, not specs
  - Spotify Wrapped: Sells vanity, not music stats

### Do (20 min)

**Activity: Ad Autopsy** 🔬
Find 3 real ads you've seen recently (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, billboards — anything). For each one:

| Question | Ad 1 | Ad 2 | Ad 3 |
|----------|------|------|------|
| Brand/product | | | |
| Where did you see it? | | | |
| What emotion does it trigger? | | | |
| Which psychological trigger does it use? | | | |
| Would your friends fall for it? Why? | | | |
| Rating: How effective is it? (1–5) | | | |

**Activity: Pick Your Product** 🎯
Choose what you'll be marketing for the rest of this path:
- **Option A**: Your startup from the Startup path (if you did it)
- **Option B**: A real brand you think could market better
- **Option C**: Invent a fictional product right now

Write a 1-sentence description:
> [Product name] is a [what it is] for [who it's for] that [what it does].

Example:
> **StudyBuddy** is an app for high school students that matches you with serious study partners based on subjects and schedule.

**Activity: Customer Desire Map** 💭
For your product's customer, fill out:

| Layer | Question | Your Answer |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Surface want** | What do they SAY they want? | |
| **Actual need** | What do they ACTUALLY need? | |
| **Deep desire** | What do they secretly FEEL? (status, belonging, safety, freedom) | |

Example for Nike:
- Surface: "I want good running shoes"
- Actual need: Comfortable shoes that last
- Deep desire: "I want to feel like an athlete"

> Great marketing speaks to the deep desire, not the surface want.

## Day 2 — 🎨 Build Your Brand

**Theme**: A brand isn't a logo. It's a feeling.

**The Hook**
> "Close your eyes and think 'Apple.' Now think 'Samsung.' Different feelings, right? That's branding. Not the logo — the FEELING. Today you build yours."

### Learn (5 min)
- **Brand ≠ Logo**. Brand = the feeling people get when they think of you
- The 3 pillars of a brand:
  - **Personality**: If your brand were a person, who would they be?
  - **Voice**: How does your brand talk? (casual? bold? nerdy? warm?)
  - **Visual identity**: Colors, fonts, style — the vibe
- Brand positioning: Where do you sit vs. competitors?
- Examples:
  - Duolingo: Chaotic, funny, guilt-trips you (on purpose)
  - Notion: Clean, minimal, intellectual
  - Red Bull: Extreme, adventurous, rebellious

### Do (25 min)

**Activity: Brand Personality Card** 🃏
Define your brand's personality:

| Question | Your Answer |
|----------|-------------|
| If your brand were a person, what's their age? | |
| What do they wear? | |
| What music do they listen to? | |
| What's their vibe in 3 words? (e.g., bold, playful, nerdy) | |
| What do they NEVER do? (e.g., "never boring", "never corporate") | |
| Celebrity / character your brand vibes with? | |

**Activity: Brand Voice Guide** 🗣️
Write your brand's voice rules:

| We ARE... | We are NOT... |
|-----------|--------------|
| (e.g., playful) | (e.g., childish) |
| (e.g., direct) | (e.g., rude) |
| (e.g., confident) | (e.g., arrogant) |

Now write the SAME message in your brand voice vs. a boring corporate voice:

| Corporate version | Your brand's version |
|---|---|
| "We are pleased to announce the launch of our new feature." | [rewrite in your voice] |
| "Please contact our support team for assistance." | [rewrite in your voice] |
| "Thank you for your purchase." | [rewrite in your voice] |

**Activity: Visual Mood Board** 🎨
Create a mini mood board for your brand (describe or sketch):
- **2 colors** that represent your brand (and WHY those colors)
- **Font vibe**: Modern/clean? Handwritten? Bold? Retro?
- **Photo style**: Bright and colorful? Dark and moody? Minimal? Chaotic?
- Find or describe **3 reference images** that capture your brand's aesthetic

**Activity: Positioning Map** 📍
Place your brand on these two axes:

```
         Premium

  Serious --+-- Playful
            |
        Affordable
```

Where does your brand sit? Where do competitors sit? Is there an empty space you can own?

## Day 3 — 📱 Content That Hits

**Theme**: Good content gets liked. Great content gets shared.

**The Hook**
> "You scroll past 300+ pieces of content every day. You remember maybe 3. Today you learn what makes those 3 unforgettable — and you'll make content that does the same."

### Learn (5 min)
- The **Content Pyramid**: Not all content has the same job
  - **Top of funnel** (awareness): Get strangers to notice you (reels, memes, viral content)
  - **Middle of funnel** (consideration): Get interested people to trust you (how-tos, testimonials, comparisons)
  - **Bottom of funnel** (conversion): Get ready buyers to take action (offers, demos, limited deals)
- **The Hook Rule**: You have 1.5 seconds. If your first line doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.
- Content formats ranked by engagement for teens:
  1. Short video (TikTok/Reels) — highest engagement
  2. Carousel posts — high save rate
  3. Memes — high share rate
  4. Stories — high for loyalty
  5. Long-form (blog/YouTube) — high for authority

### Do (25 min)

**Activity: Hook Workshop** 🪝
Write 5 different hooks for your product. Each must make someone stop scrolling:

| Hook type | Your hook |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Shocking stat** | "Did you know [surprising number]..." |
| **Controversial take** | "[Unpopular opinion about your industry]..." |
| **Before/after** | "I used to [old way]. Then I [new way]. Here's what happened..." |
| **Question** | "Why does nobody talk about [hidden problem]?" |
| **Story** | "Last week I [relatable situation]..." |

Rate each hook: Would YOU stop scrolling for this? (1–5)

**Activity: Create 3 Pieces of Content** 📝
Make 3 different content pieces for your brand. Each must be a different format:

**Piece 1 — Short-form video script** (TikTok/Reel, 30 sec)
- Write a shot-by-shot script:
  - Hook (first 2 seconds — what's on screen + what you say)
  - Body (what you show/explain)
  - CTA (what you want them to do: follow, comment, visit link)
- Include: text overlay suggestions, music vibe, transitions

**Piece 2 — Carousel post** (4–6 slides)
- Slide 1: Hook headline (the only slide people see before swiping)
- Slides 2–5: Value content (teach something, show a process, share tips)
- Last slide: CTA (follow, save, share, visit link)
- Write the caption too (first line = another hook)

**Piece 3 — Meme or relatable post**
- Create a meme that relates to your product's problem space
- Format: image concept + text overlay + caption
- Must be funny AND relevant — not just random humor
- Could be: relatable situation, before/after, expectation vs. reality

**Activity: Content Calendar Sketch** 📅
Plan 1 week of content (7 posts). For each day, write:

| Day | Format | Topic | Funnel stage | Goal |
|-----|--------|-------|-------------|------|
| Mon | | | | |
| Tue | | | | |
| Wed | | | | |
| Thu | | | | |
| Fri | | | | |
| Sat | | | | |
| Sun | | | | |

Rule: You need at least 2 top-of-funnel, 3 middle, and 1 bottom-of-funnel posts.

### Deliver
- [ ] 5 hooks (with self-ratings)
- [ ] 3 content pieces (video script, carousel, meme)
- [ ] 1-week content calendar

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Write 5 hooks | +30 |
| Create video script | +40 |
| Create carousel post | +40 |
| Create meme/relatable post | +30 |
| Content calendar complete | +20 |
| At least 1 hook rated 5/5 by you | +10 bonus |
| Content calendar has correct funnel mix | +20 bonus |

**Brand Power**: +15 if content matches your Day 2 brand voice, -10 if content feels like a different brand

## Day 4 — 📊 Read the Numbers

**Theme**: Gut feeling is for amateurs. Data is for marketers who win.

**The Hook**
> "Here's a secret: most viral content isn't lucky. It's engineered. The creators just know how to read the data and double down on what works. Today you learn to think like them."

### Learn (5 min)
- **Key metrics** every marketer needs to know:
  - **Reach**: How many people SAW your content
  - **Engagement rate**: (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach × 100
  - **CTR** (Click-Through Rate): How many people clicked your link ÷ how many saw it
  - **Conversion rate**: How many people took the action you wanted ÷ total visitors
  - **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost): Total marketing spend ÷ new customers gained
  - **ROI** (Return on Investment): (Revenue - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100
- Why **vanity metrics** lie:
  - 10,000 likes but 0 sales = bad marketing
  - 200 likes but 50 sales = great marketing
- Key concept: **A/B Testing** — test 2 versions, keep the winner, kill the loser

### Do (25 min)

**Activity: Dashboard Reading** 📈
Here's fake analytics data for a brand's Instagram account last month. Analyze it:

| Post | Type | Reach | Likes | Comments | Shares | Saves | Link clicks |
|------|------|-------|-------|----------|--------|-------|-------------|
| A | Reel | 12,400 | 890 | 45 | 210 | 65 | 30 |
| B | Carousel | 3,200 | 280 | 92 | 35 | 310 | 85 |
| C | Meme | 28,500 | 2,100 | 180 | 890 | 20 | 5 |
| D | Product photo | 1,800 | 95 | 8 | 3 | 12 | 120 |
| E | Story (poll) | 4,600 | — | 380 responses | 12 | — | 45 |

Answer these questions:
1. Calculate the engagement rate for Post A, B, and C
2. Which post drove the most AWARENESS? Why?
3. Which post drove the most CONVERSIONS (link clicks)? Why?
4. Which post should the brand make MORE of? Which should they stop?
5. The meme went viral but got only 5 link clicks. Is that a problem? Why or why not?

**Activity: A/B Test Design** 🧪
Design an A/B test for your brand. Pick ONE element to test:

| Element to test | Version A | Version B |
|----------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Headline** | [your original] | [alternative] |
| OR **Image style** | [describe style A] | [describe style B] |
| OR **CTA** | [your original CTA] | [alternative CTA] |
| OR **Posting time** | [time A] | [time B] |

For your test, define:
- **Hypothesis**: "I think Version [A/B] will perform better because..."
- **Metric**: What number will you look at to decide the winner?
- **Sample size**: How many people need to see each version before you decide?
- **Timeline**: How long will you run the test?

**Activity: Budget Scenario** 💸
You have a marketing budget of **฿5,000** for one month. Allocate it:

| Channel | Budget (฿) | Why this amount? | Expected result |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Instagram/TikTok ads | | | |
| Influencer collab | | | |
| Content creation tools | | | |
| Giveaway/contest prizes | | | |
| Other: ___ | | | |
| **Total** | **฿5,000** | | |

Rules:
- You must use at least 2 channels
- You must justify every baht
- Calculate your expected CAC: Budget ÷ expected new customers = ฿___ per customer

### Deliver
- [ ] Dashboard analysis (5 questions answered)
- [ ] A/B test design (with hypothesis)
- [ ] Budget allocation with CAC calculation

### XP & Gamification
| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Analyze dashboard (all 5 questions) | +50 |

## Day 5 — 🚀 Launch Your Campaign

**Theme**: Plans are worthless. Campaigns are everything.

**The Hook**
> "You know the psychology. You've built the brand. You've created the content. You've learned the data. Today it all comes together — you launch a real campaign."

### Learn (5 min)
- **What is a campaign?** A coordinated push with a clear goal, timeline, and message
- Campaign anatomy:
  - **Objective**: What specific outcome? (awareness, signups, sales, downloads)
  - **Target audience**: Exactly who sees this?
  - **Key message**: What's the ONE thing you want them to remember?
  - **Creative**: The content (ads, posts, videos)
  - **Channels**: Where it runs
  - **Timeline**: When it starts and ends
  - **Budget**: How much you spend
  - **KPIs**: How you know if it worked
- Campaign types teens can relate to:
  - Product launch (new drop)
  - Awareness campaign (cause marketing)
  - Seasonal campaign (back to school, summer)
  - Challenge/UGC campaign (get users to create content)

### Do (30 min)

**Activity: Campaign Brief** 📋
Write a professional campaign brief for your brand:

| Section | Your Campaign |
|---------|--------------|
| **Campaign name** | Give it a catchy internal name |
| **Objective** | 1 specific goal (e.g., "Get 200 signups in 2 weeks") |
| **Target audience** | Who exactly? (use your Day 1 persona) |
| **Key message** | The ONE sentence people should remember |
| **Channels** | Where will this campaign live? (pick 2–3) |
| **Timeline** | Start date → end date (plan for 2 weeks) |
| **Budget** | Total budget + breakdown per channel |
| **KPIs** | 3 numbers you'll track to measure success |

**Activity: Campaign Content Kit** 🎨
Create the actual content for your campaign. Must include ALL of these:

**1. Hero post** (the main campaign announcement)
- Platform: [choose one]
- Format: [video script / carousel / static image]
- Full caption with hashtags
- Visual description

**2. Supporting post** (reinforces the campaign mid-way)
- Different angle on the same message
- Different format from the hero post

**3. Urgency post** (final push — last chance / ending soon)
- Creates FOMO
- Clear final CTA

**Activity: Launch Day Timeline** ⏰
Plan the exact sequence for your campaign launch day:

| Time | Action | Channel | Content |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 8:00 AM | | | |
| 10:00 AM | | | |
| 12:00 PM | | | |
| 3:00 PM | | | |
| 6:00 PM | | | |
| 9:00 PM | | | |

Think about: When is your audience most active? What order builds momentum?

**Activity: Crisis Scenario** 🚨
It's Day 3 of your campaign. Something goes wrong. Pick a scenario and write your response:

> **Scenario A**: Someone influential posts that your product is "a scam" — it's getting shared
> **Scenario B**: Your campaign content accidentally offends a group of people — comments are blowing up
> **Scenario C**: A competitor launches the exact same campaign the same week

For your chosen scenario:
1. What's your FIRST action? (within 1 hour)
2. What's your public response? (write the actual message)
3. What do you change about the campaign going forward?

## Day 6 — 📈 Results & Your Marketing Future

**Theme**: The finale — measure, learn, and discover your career

**The Hook**
> "Campaigns end. Brands don't. Today you'll analyze your results, see what kind of marketer you are, and find out if this is the career that gets you out of bed."

### Learn (5 min)
- **Post-campaign analysis**: What to look at when a campaign ends
  - Did you hit your KPIs?
  - What content performed best? Worst?
  - What surprised you?
  - What would you do differently with 2x the budget?
- **Marketing career paths** — it's not just one job:
  - **Brand Manager**: Owns the brand identity and strategy
  - **Content Creator**: Makes the actual content (video, writing, design)
  - **Growth Marketer**: Runs experiments to grow users fast (data-heavy)
  - **Social Media Manager**: Runs brand accounts, engages with community
  - **Copywriter**: Writes ads, emails, scripts — words that sell
  - **Media Buyer**: Manages ad budgets and targeting (math-heavy)
  - **PR / Communications**: Manages reputation and media relationships
  - **Market Researcher**: Studies consumers and trends (psychology-heavy)

### Do (30 min)

2. Your reach was under target but link clicks exceeded — what does this tell you?

3. **Data isn't boring** — frame analytics as "reading the matrix" / detective work
4. **No perfect answers** — marketing is about testing, failing fast, and iterating
5. **Career breadcrumbs** — each day subtly exposes a different marketing specialization
