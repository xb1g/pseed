# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Online visitors browsing the PassionSeed hackathon gallery after the event. Mix of potential customers, mentors, judges, and curious onlookers discovering student-built health and wellness products. Primarily desktop and mobile web, at their own pace, not under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

PassionSeed is an educational platform centered on hackathons where high school and university students build real health-tech products. The gallery showcases these products to the outside world. The whale matcher quiz connects visitors to the product most relevant to their life situation — making the students' work feel real and personally meaningful, not just a school project.

## Brand Personality

Playful, warm, real. The whale mascot is friendly and approachable. The students' work is taken seriously — the tone says "these solve real problems" without being corporate. Humor is gentle (a whale helping you find things), never ironic or detached.

## Anti-references

- **BuzzFeed quizzes**: cheap, ad-laden, clickbait personality tests. No "Which character are you?" energy.
- **Corporate onboarding wizards**: step 1 of 4, progress bars, enterprise checkbox flows. This should feel like a conversation, not a form.
- **Generic SaaS landing pages**: no hero metrics, no "trusted by 10,000+ users", no gradient text.

## Design Principles

1. **The students' work is the star.** Every design choice serves the goal of connecting visitors to real products built by real students. The whale is a guide, not the main event.
2. **Conversation over configuration.** The quiz should feel like chatting with a friendly character, not filling out a form. Minimal chrome, maximum personality.
3. **Honest and specific.** No marketing fluff. The "why" text explains the match in concrete terms tied to the visitor's actual answers.
4. **Native to the ocean.** The Bloom design system (deep navy, steel-blue accents, grain textures, subtle glow) is the visual home. New surfaces extend it, never fight it.
5. **Accessible by default.** Thai and English bilingual. Touch-friendly on mobile. Reduced motion respected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Bilingual: Thai (primary audience) and English
- Touch targets ≥ 48px on mobile
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all animations
- Color contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text
