# Radar Design

Radar pages cover Career Radar and Faculty Radar. They should feel like one editorial system: atmospheric, grainy, direct, and designed for students making a real decision.

## Typography

- English titles: `Instrument Serif`
- Thai titles: `Noto Serif Thai`
- Body text, Thai and English: `Athiti`
- Faculty Radar should not use pixel or monospace fonts. Keep labels, metadata, percentages, and utility text in `Athiti`.

Use the local CSS variables instead of raw font names:

```css
--font-instrument-serif
--font-noto-serif-thai
--font-athiti
```

## Faculty Radar Structure

Faculty detail pages should answer what a student will face after entering the faculty. Keep the page focused on four sections:

1. What You Actually Study
2. Assessment Style
3. Difference From Similar Faculty
4. Career Doors After This Faculty

## Faculty Radar Type Roles

- Gallery English title, faculty English title: Instrument Serif
- Faculty Thai title and section Thai headings: Noto Serif Thai
- Descriptions, summaries, card body, notes, and long explanations: Athiti
- Percentages, small category labels, and compact metadata: Athiti

## Visual Tone

- Keep the same grainy gradient atmosphere as Career Radar.
- Cards may be bold and graphic, but the reading page should stay clear enough for comparison.
- Avoid adding more sections unless they directly help the student decide whether the faculty environment fits them.
