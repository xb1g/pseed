# ProjectSeed Minimum Viable Safeguarding Design

**Issue:** PS-190  
**Status:** Approved 2026-07-28

## Context

ProjectSeed pairs university-age alumni mentors with high-school students over a
period of months. Some students are minors. This creates a duty of care distinct
from privacy compliance and makes clear boundaries, visible communication, and
fast escalation prerequisites to Batch 1.

The design is intentionally operational and small. It establishes the minimum
controls required before selling a seat without attempting to replace a
specialist safeguarding review or a later, more mature safeguarding programme.

## Approach

Create one self-contained operational policy at
`docs/project/PROJECTSEED-SAFEGUARDING.md`. Keeping the policy, mentor code,
communication rules, response procedure, briefing, acknowledgement, and launch
gate together makes the package easier to brief, sign, and use during an
incident.

Link the policy from the ProjectSeed strategy and the repository documentation
index. Do not add application UI or automated enforcement in PS-190.

## Scope and definitions

- Apply the policy to founders, staff, alumni mentors, volunteers, contractors,
  and other adults acting for ProjectSeed.
- Treat anyone under 18 as a minor for this policy.
- When a participant's age is unknown, apply the minor safeguards until it is
  confirmed.
- Apply the communication boundaries to online and in-person ProjectSeed
  activity, including contact that begins through the programme.

## Policy contents

The operational policy will contain:

1. A commitment to students' safety and best interests.
2. A mentor code of conduct covering respectful, non-discriminatory,
   age-appropriate behaviour and explicit prohibited conduct.
3. An absolute prohibition on 1:1 private direct messages with minors. Mentoring
   must use an official group channel or include a second authorized adult.
4. Parent-visible communication: before mentoring starts, a parent or guardian
   receives the mentor identity, official channels, expected contact pattern,
   this policy, and the reporting route. Communication records are available to
   the parent or guardian on request, subject to protecting other students'
   privacy.
5. A named safeguarding lead: Bunyasit Fang, reachable at
   `seedpassion@gmail.com`.
6. A response procedure that prioritizes immediate safety, preserves evidence,
   limits disclosure to people who need to act, prevents internal amateur
   investigation, and suspends the adult's student contact while a concern is
   assessed.
7. A short briefing and signed acknowledgement required before any mentor is
   assigned to a student.
8. A Batch 1 launch gate checklist covering parent notice, approved channels,
   briefing records, signed acknowledgements, and escalation readiness.

## Important boundaries

The policy will:

- forbid sexual or romantic behaviour, grooming, harassment, humiliation,
  physical punishment, discrimination, secrecy, retaliation, substance use
  while responsible for students, private meetings, unauthorized transport or
  accommodation, off-platform contact, and undocumented personal gifts or
  financial exchanges;
- tell mentors to listen calmly to a disclosure, avoid leading questions, never
  promise secrecy, record facts, and report immediately;
- state that suspected criminal conduct or immediate danger may require
  external authorities or emergency services;
- distinguish safeguarding records from ordinary mentor notes and restrict
  their access.

It will not claim that signing a policy eliminates risk, provide legal advice,
or define detailed investigation procedures beyond the team's current
competence.
