# ProjectSeed — Minimum Viable Safeguarding Policy

**Status:** ACTIVE — REQUIRED FOR BATCH 1

**Policy owner and Safeguarding Lead:** Bunyasit Fang

**Report concerns:** `seedpassion@gmail.com`

**Effective:** 2026-07-28

**Review:** Before Batch 1 mentoring starts, after its first 30 days, and after
any safeguarding concern or material change to programme channels

This is ProjectSeed's minimum operational policy for keeping students safe while
they work with alumni mentors. It is a duty-of-care policy, separate from
privacy and PDPA compliance. It does not replace legal advice, safer recruitment,
or a future specialist safeguarding review.

> **Non-negotiable: No 1:1 private DMs with minors.** Mentoring must happen in
> an official ProjectSeed group channel or with a second authorized adult
> present. A student's consent does not create an exception.

## 1. Scope and definitions

This policy applies to founders, staff, alumni mentors, volunteers, contractors,
guests, and any other adult acting for ProjectSeed.

For this policy:

- a **minor** is any participant under 18;
- a **student** includes any young person participating in or considering
  ProjectSeed;
- an **authorized adult** is an adult approved by the Safeguarding Lead and
  briefed on this policy;
- an **official channel** is a ProjectSeed-approved group space whose members
  and message history can be reviewed by ProjectSeed;
- a **safeguarding concern** is any suspicion, disclosure, allegation, boundary
  breach, or observed behaviour that may indicate a student is unsafe or at
  risk of physical, sexual, emotional, online, or other harm.

When a participant's age is unknown, everyone must apply the safeguards for
minors until the age is confirmed. These rules cover online and in-person
activity, including contact that began through ProjectSeed but continues
elsewhere.

## 2. Responsibilities

Everyone covered by this policy must:

1. put the student's safety and best interests first;
2. maintain visible, age-appropriate professional boundaries;
3. follow the communication rules in this policy;
4. report concerns and boundary breaches immediately; and
5. cooperate with protective action while preserving the student's dignity and
   privacy.

The Safeguarding Lead must:

- make this policy available to students, parents or guardians, and mentors;
- approve mentoring channels and authorized adults;
- maintain briefing and acknowledgement records;
- receive and securely record concerns;
- take immediate protective action and decide when external referral is needed;
- prevent retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith; and
- review the policy and its operation at the stated review points.

If a concern involves the Safeguarding Lead, or the lead cannot be reached
during an urgent risk, the reporter must contact an appropriate external child
protection or law-enforcement authority directly. Immediate danger takes
priority over internal reporting.

## 3. Mentor code of conduct

Every mentor must read, discuss, and sign the acknowledgement in section 10
before being assigned to a student.

### Mentors must always

- treat every student fairly and with respect, regardless of identity,
  background, disability, beliefs, or circumstances;
- use language and activities appropriate to the student's age and maturity;
- listen to students without ridicule, coercion, or promises that cannot be
  kept;
- keep ProjectSeed interactions observable and inside approved times, places,
  and channels;
- make goals, feedback, schedule changes, and follow-up actions visible in the
  official group channel;
- protect student information and share it only with people who need it to
  support or safeguard the student;
- maintain accurate factual records of attendance, agreed actions, and any
  concern; and
- report any concern or policy breach immediately, even when the available
  information is incomplete or the alleged person is a friend or team member.

### Mentors must never

- engage in sexual, romantic, flirtatious, grooming, or exploitative behaviour
  with a student;
- initiate or continue 1:1 private DMs with a minor;
- move ProjectSeed communication to a personal or disappearing-message account,
  or delete communication to hide it;
- ask a student to keep a relationship, message, meeting, gift, or concern
  secret from their parent, guardian, or ProjectSeed;
- request sexual content, private images, unnecessary personal information, or
  detailed disclosures of trauma or abuse;
- shame, threaten, bully, discriminate against, humiliate, blackmail, or
  physically punish a student;
- use unnecessary physical contact or engage a student in an unsafe activity;
- meet a minor alone in a private or isolated place, including a private video
  call;
- transport, host, or arrange accommodation for a minor without advance written
  approval from the Safeguarding Lead and the parent or guardian;
- give or accept personal money, loans, paid work, or significant gifts outside
  a documented programme process;
- provide or encourage alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, illegal drugs, or
  other age-restricted products, or be impaired while responsible for students;
- take, publish, or share a student's image, story, project, contact details, or
  identifying information without the required student and parent or guardian
  permission; or
- ignore, conceal, discourage, or retaliate against a safeguarding report.

A boundary breach must be reported even if no harm is known to have occurred.
The Safeguarding Lead may pause or end a mentor's involvement for breaching this
code.

## 4. Communication and meeting rules

### Online

- A mentoring group channel must include the student, mentor, and at least one
  second authorized adult.
- Project decisions and meaningful mentoring exchanges must stay in that
  channel so the record is visible and reviewable.
- A live call with a minor must use an approved link, be scheduled in the group
  channel, and include a second authorized adult who is actually present.
- Mentors must not add minors from ProjectSeed as personal social-media contacts
  or continue programme relationships through personal accounts.
- If a minor sends a private DM, the mentor must not continue the mentoring
  conversation there. The mentor must redirect the student to the official
  group channel and notify the second authorized adult.
- If the private message indicates immediate danger, the mentor may respond only
  as needed to support immediate safety, must preserve the message, and must
  report it to the Safeguarding Lead immediately.

### In person

- Mentoring must take place in a scheduled group setting or an open, observable
  location with a second authorized adult present.
- Doors must remain open where practical, and the mentor must not create an
  isolated one-adult/one-minor situation.
- Schedule or location changes must be posted in the official group channel.
- Transport, home visits, and overnight activity are not part of minimum viable
  ProjectSeed mentoring.

## 5. Parent-visible communication

Before a minor's mentoring begins, ProjectSeed must send the parent or guardian:

- the mentor's name and role;
- the approved channels, expected contact pattern, and meeting format;
- this policy and the no-private-DM rule;
- the Safeguarding Lead's name and reporting email; and
- a clear statement that they may raise a concern without retaliation.

The parent or guardian must acknowledge receipt before mentor contact starts.
ProjectSeed must make communication concerning their child available on request.
When a group record includes other students' private information, ProjectSeed
will provide the requesting parent with their child's relevant record or a
redacted copy rather than disclose another student's information.

Mentors must not create a separate communication arrangement with a minor
because a parent is busy, does not use the platform, or verbally permits it.
ProjectSeed must choose a visible alternative.

## 6. How to report a concern

Students, parents or guardians, mentors, and team members may report any concern
directly to:

**Bunyasit Fang — ProjectSeed Safeguarding Lead**

**Email:** `seedpassion@gmail.com`

Reports should state only the facts currently known: who was involved, what
happened or was observed, when and where it happened, and whether anyone may be
in immediate danger. Do not circulate the report in a general group channel.

Report:

- **immediately** when a student may be in danger, a disclosure is made, or
  possible abuse, grooming, exploitation, or criminal conduct is suspected; and
- **on the same day** for a boundary breach or other policy concern.

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service
or external authority first, then notify the Safeguarding Lead as soon as it is
safe. Anyone may report directly to an external authority; ProjectSeed
permission is not required.

## 7. Receiving a disclosure

When a student shares that they or another person may have been harmed:

1. **Listen calmly.** Take the student seriously and let them use their own
   words.
2. **Prioritize safety.** Ask only what is necessary to understand whether
   someone is in immediate danger.
3. **Do not promise secrecy.** Explain that you will tell only people who need
   to help keep them safe.
4. **Do not investigate.** Do not ask leading questions, confront the alleged
   person, seek proof, or contact witnesses.
5. **Record facts promptly.** Write the student's words as accurately as
   possible, along with date, time, place, people present, and actions taken.
   Separate observations from assumptions.
6. **Preserve evidence.** Keep relevant messages, screenshots, or files intact;
   do not edit, forward broadly, or delete them.
7. **Report immediately** to the Safeguarding Lead, or directly to an external
   authority when required for immediate safety or because the concern involves
   the lead.

## 8. ProjectSeed response

On receiving a concern, the Safeguarding Lead must:

1. check immediate safety and arrange urgent external help when needed;
2. acknowledge the report and create a restricted safeguarding record;
3. pause the subject adult's student contact and channel access when reasonably
   necessary as a precaution, without treating the pause as a finding of fact;
4. preserve records and limit information to people who need it to protect the
   student or meet legal obligations;
5. refer suspected criminal conduct or serious risk to the appropriate external
   authority and cooperate with lawful instructions;
6. inform the parent or guardian unless doing so may increase risk to the
   student or conflict with external-authority guidance;
7. document decisions, actions, owners, and follow-up dates; and
8. check that the reporter and student are protected from retaliation.

ProjectSeed must not run an amateur investigation or ask a student to repeat a
disclosure to multiple team members. Safeguarding records must be stored
separately from ordinary mentor notes with access restricted to the Safeguarding
Lead and people who need the information to act.

## 9. Required mentor briefing

Before any alumnus or other adult mentors a student, the Safeguarding Lead or an
authorized briefer must conduct a short live briefing covering:

1. why ProjectSeed has a duty of care and who counts as a minor;
2. the no-1:1-private-DM rule, including what to do when a student initiates one;
3. approved channels, calls, meetings, and parent visibility;
4. prohibited conduct and realistic boundary scenarios;
5. how to receive a disclosure without investigating;
6. how and when to report to Bunyasit Fang at `seedpassion@gmail.com`; and
7. questions, confirmation of understanding, and signature of section 10.

The briefing record must contain the mentor's name, date, briefer, questions or
clarifications, and acknowledgement status. ProjectSeed must keep the signed
acknowledgement in a restricted operational folder. No briefing and signature
means no student assignment.

## 10. Mentor acknowledgement

```text
ProjectSeed Mentor Safeguarding Acknowledgement

I have read and discussed the ProjectSeed Minimum Viable Safeguarding Policy.
I understand that I must not communicate with a minor through 1:1 private DMs,
meet a minor privately, or move ProjectSeed contact outside approved visible
channels. I agree to follow the mentor code of conduct, protect student privacy,
report concerns and boundary breaches immediately, and cooperate with protective
action.

I understand that ProjectSeed may pause or end my mentor role if I breach this
policy and may refer suspected illegal conduct to external authorities.

Mentor full name:
Signature:
Date:
Briefed by:
Briefing date:
```

An electronic signature or written confirmation is acceptable only if
ProjectSeed retains an attributable, dated copy.

## 12. Review and limitations

The Safeguarding Lead must review this policy:

- before Batch 1 mentoring begins;
- 30 days after the first mentor contact;
- after any concern, near miss, or boundary breach;
- when the programme, mentor model, age range, channels, or in-person activity
  changes; and
- when legal or specialist guidance changes.

Batch 1 review must specifically consider independent safeguarding advice, safer
mentor recruitment and screening, a named deputy lead, student-friendly and Thai
translations, record retention, and whether technical channel controls are
needed.

This policy establishes minimum conduct and response rules. It does not prove
that a person is safe, guarantee that harm cannot occur, or determine criminal
or civil liability.
