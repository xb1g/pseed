## What we are actually doing

**Purpose:** guide the new generation to design their own future so they can live fully.

**The thesis, in one sentence:** the Thai admission system is pay-to-win and cram-and-forget,
but the TCAS Round 1 portfolio project is the single window in a student's entire education
where they are permitted to build something real — and we occupy that window and refuse to
let it be filled with a fake proof-of-concept.

We are not selling admissions help with education smuggled inside. The alignment between
what we sell (relief from admissions panic) and what we deliver (a real self-authored
project) is genuine.

**What we sell to parents:** their child ships a real project, with real users interviewed,
documented, in time to matter.

**What actually changes the student:** the project, and a community where building is normal.

## Where this came from

18 months. Four products. ~1,300 students. Traction, no growth.

| Product | Reach | Money | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| TechSeed | ~500 | 100฿, one paid batch, multi-week | Alumni changed. Bonds formed. Some came back to teach. |
| BizSeed | — | ~2,990฿, one day, onsite Chula | Cleared easily. Students don't stick around. |
| Hackathon | 800+ | free | Design thinking, students interviewed real users. Format works. |
| Passion Map | — | never priced | Untested commercially |
| Radar | — | never priced | Untested commercially |

**Diagnosis:** we built supply-side products in a demand-side market, and never captured
outcome data on any of it. The market told us what it values — 30x more money for 1/10th
the delivery time — and we kept building the thing nobody was asked to pay for.

**The growth blocker is delivery capacity, and the founder is the capacity.**

## The two discoveries that reshaped this

**1. Alumni come back to teach.** Students who arrived at zero returned as teachers.
Nobody asked them to. At least 5 have prior teaching experience from past TechSeed cohorts;
**Kyzz** asked directly to build TechSeed with us. This is unfakeable proof of trajectory
change AND the only capacity-scaling path that doesn't dilute what makes the program work.

**2. The gap isn't credentials — it's community.** Alumni who reach university stop
building. Absorbed by pub, games, uni activities, the next test. Not from lost interest —
from lost context. Nobody around them treats building as normal.

> "they lack communities for passion + project + values to keep them going and building
> something valuable to them"

The camp is the entry event. **The community is the product.** That is why there is no
calendar deadline: we are selling membership in the only room where this is normal.

> **Amended 2026-07-29 — batch 1 only.** Selling to M6 makes an open-ended membership
> impossible: the value is delivered before a fixed external submission date or it is not
> delivered. Batch 1 is a time-boxed sprint back-solved from TCAS Round 1. The no-deadline
> design survives for M4-M5 and for batch 2.
>
> Say the cost out loud: **M6 churns roughly four months after admissions, so batch 1 does
> not test the community thesis.** We are running the segment with the clearest urgency and
> the weakest fit to the thing we claim is the product. That is a knowing trade, made because
> a segment with no deadline could not be sold to at all (see "Batch 1 audience").

## The offer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | ProjectSeed — named for the artifact, not the audience. TechSeed / BizSeed become entry events. |
| **Price** | 2,990฿, single tier. **No founding promo** — removed 2026-07-29. Explicitly a *validation* price; batch 2 pricing is unpromised in writing to every batch-1 buyer. |
| **Only discount** | Referral, and **credit is earned only for referring M4-M5 juniors** — never M6 peers. See "Batch 1 audience" below. |
| **Included** | Community, guidance, Radar, Passion Map, curriculum, group sessions. |
| **Paid mentorship tiers** | **None.** Removed deliberately — see below. |
| **Access** | 10% scholarship seats every batch. |
| **Guarantee** | The project ships, documented, with real users interviewed. **No admission guarantee.** |
| **Batch 1 deadline** | **Time-boxed to the TCAS Round 1 submission window.** This reverses the original "no calendar deadline" for batch 1 only — see below. |
| **Refund** | 100% until the student submits deliverable #1, 0% after. Behavioural gate, not calendar. |
| **Mentors** | Alumni (5+, incl. Kyzz). Not hiring. |
| **Marketing** | Publish student projects and alumni outcomes as a counter-rubric to pay-to-win competitions. |

**Why there are no paid mentorship tiers.** Selling mentorship separately broke the product
twice: richer students would buy better projects (reproducing the exact pay-to-win system we
exist to oppose), and the guarantee's critical input would have been an optional upsell
(meaning the base tier could not deliver what it promised). One price with guidance included
dissolves both.

**Why we guarantee the project and not admission.** We do not control the admission
committee, and by our own assessment that system is rigged. A shipped project is true
regardless of who reads it.

**Founding batch, not discount.** Founding = scarcity and status. Discount = everyone learns
the real price is the low one, forever.

> **Amended 2026-07-29 — the founding promo is removed entirely.** The argument above was
> right and was applied one step further: discounting to a parent whose child has a live
> deadline signals doubt, and in that segment price is read as a trust signal. There is no
> 1,990฿ tier. Still ask every buyer what they thought they were buying and what they would
> have paid — that is now the only source of price data we have.

### Batch 1 audience *(added 2026-07-29)*

**M6 primary. M4-M5 accepted. Grade 9 (M3) out. Alumni are mentors, not buyers.**

M4-M5 was tested first and failed on the wedge: with no deadline, nothing makes a parent pay
this week rather than next term, and inventing urgency for a segment that has none is how you
end up selling scarcity theatre. M6 supplies the forcing function for free.

Charging alumni competes with recruiting them, and they are the capacity bet the whole model
rests on. They stay on the mentor side — and a recent-TCAS alum is the most credible proof
asset we have, because they just survived the thing the buyer is afraid of.

**Referral credit is earned only for referring M4-M5 juniors, never M6 peers.** M6 students
compete for the same university seats, so asking one to refer an M6 friend is asking them to
recruit a rival. Crediting junior referrals removes the rivalry and seeds batch 2 using
batch-1 buyers as the channel. The discount amount still needs re-deriving for a 2,990฿ base.

**Sales consequence:** the buyer is the parent, the doer is the student. Every page has two
readers with different fears — the parent fears wasted money and a missed deadline, the
student fears another obligation.

### Batch 1 economics, plainly

```
20 seats − 2 scholarship (10%) = 18 paying
  × 2,990฿ flat → ~53,800฿ gross, before referral discounts
```

Fine for a validation batch. Not a company yet. Whether it becomes one depends entirely on
alumni mentors becoming real capacity — which is unproven.

**Read that last sentence as a warning about the price, not a footnote.** Holding at 2,990฿
rather than raising it bets the margin on mentor-hours-per-student, which risk 1 says is
unmeasured, delivered by alumni capacity, which risk 5 says is unproven. That is acceptable
for a validation batch on exactly one condition, now binding: **2,990฿ is a validation price
and batch 2 pricing is unpromised, in writing, to every batch-1 buyer.** PS-207 is what makes
batch 2 knowable.

## Open risks — read these before you disagree with a decision

1. **Mentor-hours per student is unknown.** Flagged by two reviews, still unmeasured. Without
   it, no price is knowable as profitable. Measuring it in batch 1 is PS-207.
2. **Load accumulates, revenue does not.** No deadline means batch 1 students still consume
   guidance when batch 3 starts. Alumni supply is the answer; it is a bet.
   *Amended 2026-07-29:* batch 1's TCAS deadline caps this for batch 1 only — M6 load ends at
   the submission window. It returns in full the moment M4-M5 become the primary segment, so
   this is deferred, not solved.
3. **Volunteer mentor cliff.** Unpaid alumni typically disengage around month 4-6.
4. **Community-as-product is a hypothesis.** Based on the founder's observation of alumni who
   stayed in touch. Not yet validated.
5. **Alumni-as-capacity is a hypothesis.** 5 self-selected people are not a network, and
   teaching juniors is not the same skill as supervising ambiguous self-directed work.
6. **"Shipped" is undefined.** Without a quality bar we become the credential factory we
   oppose. PS-206.
7. **Safeguarding.** University-age mentors, minors, months of contact. Duty of care,
   separate from PDPA. [The PS-190 safeguarding policy](./PROJECTSEED-SAFEGUARDING.md)
   is a hard prerequisite to selling a seat.
8. **A recognizable ProjectSeed format may read as coached** to admissions readers.
9. **M4 vs M6 are different segments.** M4 has exploration time, M6 needs an artifact now.
   ~~Deliberately deferred past batch 1.~~ *Resolved 2026-07-29: batch 1 is M6-primary. The
   new open question is whether M4-M5 sitting inside an M6-paced batch works at all — they
   have no deadline and will be surrounded by people who do.*
10. **Nine failure modes have no defined response** — ~~student never ships, refunds, promo
    price leakage,~~ parent paid but student unwilling, mentor drops mid-batch, and others.
    *2026-07-29: refunds and promo-price leakage are now decided (PS-197). Still undefined:
    student never ships; student inactive then returns; parent paid but student unwilling;
    mentor drops mid-batch.*
11. **A 4-month M6 sprint is structurally what a portfolio consultancy sells.** *(added
    2026-07-29)* The only thing distinguishing us is whether the student did the work
    themselves. That promotes risk 6 from an open item to the critical path and to the core
    of the marketing claim: without a defined quality bar (PS-206) we cannot prove we are not
    the thing we exist to oppose.
12. **The refund gate depends on an undefined artifact.** *(added 2026-07-29)* "100% until
    deliverable #1" is unbounded liability until deliverable #1 is defined, dated, and small
    enough to land in week 1. Do not sell before it exists.

## Working rules

- **PDPA:** talk to past students conversationally through Discord / LINE OA channels they
  opted into. No formal survey. Written release — parental if under 20 — before publishing
  any student story or project, including Kyzz's.
- **Safeguarding:** no 1:1 private DMs with minors. Group channels, or a second adult
  present. Follow the [operational safeguarding policy](./PROJECTSEED-SAFEGUARDING.md),
  published at `/projectseed/safeguarding`. Note that the DM rule **cannot be technically
  enforced or detected** on Discord — parent visibility is therefore a safety control, not a
  courtesy.
- **Discord:** forums for project work, group channels for chat, never DMs. Forum threads are
  persistent, attributed, and reviewable, which makes the safeguarding control and the
  build-in-public behaviour the same mechanism. Config requirements in the policy, §4.
- **Evidence:** conversational outreach finds engaged survivors and misses quiet failures.
  Use it to find mentors and warm leads. Never to substantiate a published impact claim.
- **Payments:** manual PromptPay. Sufficient to ~50 seats. Build checkout after someone pays.
- **The binding constraint is execution, not planning.** Four approved strategy docs existed
  on this branch before a single customer conversation happened. Do not add a fifth.
  *2026-07-29: a fifth was written anyway (the batch 1 design doc). It amends rather than
  replaces, and it carries a one-line assignment instead of a roadmap — but the count is now
  five, and there are still zero PromptPay receipts. The next artifact should be a receipt.*
- **Blocking cleanup:** `/techseed` is live at 1,550฿ base with a referral ladder flooring at
  950฿ (`lib/techseed/referral.ts`, mirrored by the DB trigger). ProjectSeed supersedes
  TechSeed, so that page is a public price anchor well below 2,990฿. Resolve before selling a
  seat.
