# Features

Short summary of PassionSeed products and features.

## Career Discovery

| Feature | Route | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Career Radar | `/radar` | Interactive career exploration and matching |
| Faculty Radar | `/faculty-radar` | Discover university faculties and programs |
| Direction Finder | `/direction-finder` | Background job-driven career direction suggestions *(deprecated)* |

## Learning Paths

| Feature | Route | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| PathLab | `/pathlab` | Create, manage, and browse structured learning paths |
| My Path | `/my-path` | Student-facing view of enrolled paths and progress |
| Learning Maps | `/map` | Node-based interactive learning journeys with prerequisites |
| Plan | `/plan` | Career planning wizard and goal setting |

## Hackathons & Real-World Challenges

| Feature | Route | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Hackathon | `/hackathon` | Multi-day hackathon programs for health-tech products |
| Epic Sprint | `/epic-sprint` | Compressed sprint report and showcase |
| Pitch | `/pitch` | Pitch practice and submission |

## Community & Mentorship

| Feature | Route | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Expert Interview | `/expert-interview` | AI-guided interviews with working professionals |
| Communities | `/communities` | Student communities and groups |
| Workshops | `/workshops` | Workshop discovery and enrollment |
| Teams | `/team` | Student collaboration groups inside classrooms |
| Classrooms | `/classroom` | Educator-managed learning environments |

## Tools & Utilities

| Feature | Route | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Business Model Canvas | `/business-model-canvas` | Build and refine startup BMCs |
| Reflection | `/reflection` | Personal reflection journal |
| Seeds | `/seeds` | Curated starting points / seeds of content |
| TCAS | `/tcas` | TCAS-related tools and guidance |
| CC Research | `/cc-research` | Research content hub |
| Market Research | `/market-research` | Market research tools |

## Admin

| Feature | Route | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Admin Dashboard | `/admin` | Manage users, content, hackathons, paths, and settings |

## Shared Building Blocks

- **Auth** — Supabase Auth with SSR (`/auth`, `/login`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`)
- **User Portal** — Dashboard showing next steps, reflections, workshops, and communities
- **AI Elements** — Reusable AI-assisted UI components
- **Design System** — Dawn (students) / Dusk (experts) themes, see `docs/ui-design-system.md`
