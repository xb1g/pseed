# PathLab — Product Doc

## Purpose

**Core problem.** Health-track M4 students in Thailand pick a career (med, nursing,
pharmacy, PT, etc.) with almost no real exposure to what the work actually is. PathLab
gives them a cheap, honest, hands-on way to find out — before they commit years to a
track that turns out wrong for them.

**Primary user.** M4 students exploring health careers.

**Success.** After a PathLab, a student knows whether they want to go deeper on that
track or try another one. "Not for me, moving on" is as much a win as "this is it,
let's go deeper" — either way they leave with a decision, not just information.

## Product

PathLab is a feature inside the existing PassionSeed web platform, not a standalone
product.

**Core mechanic.** An actionable, hands-on, interactive online learning experience —
not a quiz, not a static roadmap. Students *do* the real (simplified) work of a career
and see what sticks.

**Static vs. interactive.** All of it is interactive. There's no passive-browsing mode.

**Output.** Not a report or a match score. The output is reflection — on what the
student did, what stuck, what didn't — followed by a conversation with a mentor about
what's next (go deeper on this track, or try another).

## Journey

1. **Discovery** — IG video / Radar / IG viral quiz drives students in.
2. **Entry** — student sees an explanation of what PathLab is, then a list of paths to
   choose from.
3. **Middle** — hands-on activities inside the chosen path, at the student's own pace,
   like a self-paced online course.
4. **Exit** — reflection on the experience, then talking to a mentor about next steps.
   Exit is an action (talk to mentor / decide go-deeper-or-switch), not just information.

**Usage pattern.** Self-paced, like an online course. Each map/path is a one-time
experience per student — they don't repeat the same path, but they can try other paths.

## Monetization

- **Dual purpose.** Revenue generator in its own right, and a growth/engagement
  funnel into the rest of PassionSeed.
- **Direct revenue.** One-time fee per PathLab.
- **Mentor referral cut.** PathLab links students to pro educators/mentors;
  PassionSeed takes a cut of that connection.
- **B2B.** Schools/institutions can pay for access on behalf of their students.
- **Sponsorship.** Health institutions/universities can pay to be featured as a path.

## Scope

- **V1 breadth.** Launch wide — cover multiple health careers/paths at once, not one
  deep vertical slice.
- **Explicitly out of scope for V1.** Going deep into any single path. V1 is about
  breadth of exploration across tracks, not depth within one.
