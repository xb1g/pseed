## Cross-cutting patterns (the office-hours headline material)

1. **One pain rules them all: "อยากทำ แต่ไม่รู้จะเริ่มจากไหน".** ~10 of 12 leads. The reply playbook should lead with one concrete first step written for that kid — never a bare link.
2. **Bare link drops kill threads.** pat1107_nni, phuwxdx.n, peaceful22_8 all closed politely right after a naked link. A link is homework; a step is help.
3. **Group-of-4 pricing is a structural blocker.** "No friends in this field" stalled 3+ threads. Quote 299 solo by default; group rate only when they volunteer friends. (The loneliness is also unflagged `wants_community` demand.)
4. **The parent is the close.** Best lead is blocked on "ask mom" — arm kids with a forwardable parent packet instead of re-pitching the kid.
5. **Med/health is the biggest live cluster** (4 of 12: แพทย์/ทันตะ/สัตวะ/พยาบาล) and uncovered until this week's med PathLab ships. Hold with a dated promise; never "ยังไม่มีสายนั้น".
6. **Trust threads, not flags.** Classifier produced: phantom pay-ready (founder echo), `job_seeking` ม.4 students, stale `last_message_direction`, and missed community signals. Flags route; threads decide.
7. **Ops bugs are costing trust now:** duplicate auto-DM openers (potato_sweetheart8, pat1107_nni) make us look like a bot.
