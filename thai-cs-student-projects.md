## AI & Machine Learning

### 1. AI-Powered Malaria Diagnosis System (2026)
**Team:** Natdanai Suksri, Nattaphong Thaworn, Poomjai Pongsriwat
**School:** Prince Royal's College, Chiang Mai
**Award:** 1st Place Grand Award + US$6,000 at Regeneron ISEF 2026 (Phoenix, USA)

A portable, low-cost AI-powered diagnostic platform that rapidly detects all 5 major strains of malaria using smart microscopy. Designed for rural and hard-to-access communities. Also won 3 Special Awards at the same competition. Over 1,700 young scientists from 60+ countries participated.

**Source:** [HoneyKids Asia — Thai students win 1st place at ISEF 2026](https://honeykidsasia.com/thailand/thai-students-win-awards-isef-2026/)

### 2. "Prib-Ta" (พริบตา) — AI NMR Compound Identification (2026)

### 8. AR + AI Plant Recommendation App (2024)
**School:** Bangkok University
**Context:** Student innovation showcase

An application using AR to detect physical space and AI to analyze suitable plants. Users photograph their room, the app generates 3D plant models, and places them in the desired area to simulate the look before purchasing. Combines computer vision, 3D rendering, and botanical knowledge.

## Conservation Tech

### 10. "Homes for Hornbills" (2026)
**Creator:** Yanin "Proud" Tangkaravakoon (17 years old)
**Award:** Oceania & SEA Winner, The Earth Prize 2026 + National Geographic Slingshot Challenge
**Prize:** US$12,500 funding + mentorship

A project combining wildlife conservation, recycled materials, and community engagement to address declining hornbill populations in Thailand. Creates artificial nests from upcycled plastic bottles. 20 nests installed in southern Thailand, 14 hornbill chicks successfully fledged in 2025, 14 nests occupied in 2026.

**Source:** [The Earth Prize Press Release](https://theearthprize.pr.co/265176-the-100k-earth-prize-2026-17-year-old-from-thailand-named-oceania-amp-southeast-asia-winner-for-hornbill-nesting-solution)

## Key Takeaways

1. **AI + Healthcare** is the dominant theme — malaria, cancer screening, cholesterol, disease phenotyping
2. **Cross-university teams** are common and often win (Chula + Mahidol, KMITL + Kasetsart)
3. **KMITL, KMUTT, Chulalongkorn, Mahidol** are the most active CS/engineering faculties
4. **Social impact** projects (disaster response, accessibility, conservation) win more prestigious awards
5. Thai students compete successfully at international level (ISEF, WGC Japan, Berkeley SkyDeck, Earth Prize)
